## House rules — apply to every prompt

Append this block to any prompt unless the slot says otherwise:

> Cinematic defense-technology photography. Deep navy-black field (#04090F to #0B1929), cold cobalt
> light (#1A4FBA), one restrained warm accent. Long lens, shallow atmosphere, volumetric haze, high
> dynamic range with crushed blacks and no lifted shadows. Anamorphic, slightly desaturated, film
> grain barely present. Composition is calm and horizontal. Negative space is the point.

**Negative prompt, every time:**

> text, letters, words, numerals, logos, wordmarks, insignia, flags, national markings, unit patches,
> watermark, signature, UI overlays, HUD graphics, lens flare starbursts, chromatic aberration,
> people's faces, crowds, toy-like CGI, plastic sheen, orange-and-teal grade, oversaturation,
> stock-photo handshake, clipart, tilt-shift blur

**Three hard constraints, from the brand rules:**

1. **No text of any kind in the image.** Five heroes were retired in the September build because they
   had a baked-in HYPATIUS wordmark. Do not reintroduce one. The wordmark is applied in HTML, over
   the image, where it can be swapped.
2. **No real-world insignia, no identifiable aircraft or spacecraft tail numbers, no flags.** Suggest
   the domain, never a specific nation, unit or platform.
3. **Nothing that reads as a weapon effect.** Red is reserved for lethal states inside the product and
   never appears in marketing imagery.

**Grade check before you accept a render:** open it at 15% zoom. If any single element pulls the eye
before the overall shape does, it will fight the headline. Reject it.

# Slot 09 · Leadership portrait treatment  ⏸ **parked — new photography needed**

**Parked, 18 Sep 2026.** The five existing photographs were processed to this recipe and the result was
not good enough — the site keeps the originals until the team is re-shot. Do not re-attempt this on the
current photographs; the ceiling is the source material, not the treatment.

**One finding worth keeping, because it changes the brief.** `.fnd__photo` is `border-radius:50%`. These
render as **circles** at 64px (home row), 78px (company card) and 120px (lead card), and the CSS draws its
own `border:1px solid var(--slate-200)`. So the "1px hairline at the crop edge" below is wrong — it would be
clipped away entirely — and the composition target is what survives the inscribed circle, not what fills the
square. Whoever books the shoot should brief for that: head and shoulders, eyes on the upper third, nothing
important in the corners.

The recipe below stands for a re-shoot, minus the hairline:

- Square crop, eyes on the upper third line, shoulders squared to camera.
- Desaturate to roughly 25% of original chroma, then push a cool cast into the shadows only.
- Background replaced with a flat `#0E1E33` or a two-stop vertical gradient from `#14253E` to `#0B1929`.
- Identical output: 512×512, no vignette, no drop shadow.

## What the harness caught, for whoever generates the next one

`verify.mjs` cannot check a photograph. It composites *computed* CSS backgrounds, and a `background-image`
has no colour to read, so type over any image passes trivially. The instruction at the top of "Delivery back
into the site" was therefore wrong as written, and is corrected here:

**Run both harnesses after any imagery change:**

```
node verification/verify.mjs          # 96 checks — layout, type floor, targets, banned terms
node verification/verify-imagery.mjs  # 32 checks — real pixels under every headline
```

The second one is the one that can fail on a new image. It hides the text, screenshots the ground, and takes
the 95th-percentile luminance inside each headline's box — a headline is only as readable as its brightest
patch of sky. If a render puts a bright element where type sits, that is where it will show up.

Two composition rules learned from this batch, worth folding into any future prompt:

1. **State which side the veil is on, not just where the subject goes.** "Card sits right, keep the left
   open" was ambiguous enough that the ALIDADE render put its subject under the 94% end of the veil. Say
   "the right 40% is crushed to near-black and will hide anything placed there."
2. **A centred composition and a right-weighted one are not interchangeable**, even between two frames of
   the same subject. Slot 03 and Slot 10 both asked for the orbital lattice and they are not swappable —
   one has type beside it, the other has a wordmark on top of it.
