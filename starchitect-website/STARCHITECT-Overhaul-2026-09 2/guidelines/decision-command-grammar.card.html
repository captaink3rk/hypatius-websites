Decisions · resolved 16 Sep 2026

The command interface is not a mode. It is the console.

Arcade mode is retired. The grammar it carried is promoted into FUSION as first-class, always-on, namespaced components. Portrait, wireframe, command card, minimap, control groups, resources, alerts, the advisor line: all of it ships on every ops surface, in gold, cyan, mint and red on the void, with Bebas, Rajdhani and Plex Mono. There is no blue. There is no toggle. There is no training tier that gets the good console and an accreditation tier that does not.

Why

v8 got the insight right and the packaging wrong. The insight: the real-time strategy interaction model is the best-tested command interface in existence. Tens of millions of people learned to run a multi-front engagement under a decision clock through control groups, a command card and a minimap. The packaging: v8 put that grammar in skin-sc.css, gated it to body.arcade, painted it #8FC8FF and #3E5570, and labelled it "SC2 nod, never accreditation surfaces."

That framing treats the grammar as a costume. It is not. A control group on a hotkey is a faster way to select a task group than a dropdown. A command card is a faster way to see every legal order than a context menu. A minimap is a faster way to know where the theater is than a scroll. These are ergonomics, and ergonomics belong on the surface a commander actually uses. The blue was the problem. The grammar never was.

Pattern, then what it does in a space C2 console, then the brand token that carries it. Patterns are interaction conventions; no third-party art, name or trade dress is used or referenced.
