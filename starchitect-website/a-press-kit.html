01 · Boilerplate

Approved language. Use as-is.

Three variants for different lengths. Pick the version that fits the format — don't combine sentences across versions; each is internally consistent.

One sentence · 25–30 words

STARCHITECT is a full-stack BMC4I platform for CJADC2 and dynamic space operations, built by HYPATIUS LLC — a non-traditional defense contractor based in Charleston, South Carolina.

Short · 60–80 words

STARCHITECT, a HYPATIUS platform, is the operating system for space warfare and Combined Joint All-Domain Command and Control (CJADC2). It fuses aerospace-grade astrodynamics with a decentralized, survivable command-and-control mesh — engineered to fight through Denied, Degraded, Intermittent, and Limited (DDIL) network conditions while operating at machine speed. HYPATIUS LLC is a small business and non-traditional defense contractor headquartered in Charleston, South Carolina.

Long · 130–150 words

STARCHITECT is a full-stack Battle Management Command, Control, Communications, Computers, and Intelligence (BMC4I) platform built by HYPATIUS LLC. It directly addresses four U.S. national priority programs: Combined Joint All-Domain Command and Control (CJADC2), BMC4I modernization, multi-domain wargaming, and the Golden Dome homeland missile defense initiative.

The platform combines aerospace-grade astrodynamics — based on the open-source Orekit and poliastro libraries — with a decentralized Kubernetes (K3s) and NATS messaging mesh that survives kinetic, cyber, and network attack. STARCHITECT's CJADC2 MCP Server provides AI-accelerated decision support with sub-500ms multi-domain correlation, while its five-tier PACE architecture ensures mission continuity through progressive infrastructure degradation.

HYPATIUS LLC is a small business and non-traditional defense contractor headquartered in Charleston, South Carolina. UEI: UKELB3UV76V6. CAGE: 19S89.
