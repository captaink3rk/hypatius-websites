●  Visual Foundations
Build the deep. Then chart it.
Surfaces stack, never invert
Abyss is deepest; cards lift to Surface / Surface-Hi. Depth comes from elevation, not effects.
One-hue borders, alpha-stepped
A single cyan-tinted border hue at faint / medium / strong. The consistency is the system.
Glass cards · teal edge
Rounded surfaces on navy; a 2px teal left-edge marks active items (radius flattens that side).
No shadows in-product
No drop shadows, glows, or gradients in the app. The soft teal glow lives in marketing & hero art only.
4px spacing · weighted radii
4px base; rhythms of 8 / 12 / 14 / 16. Radius scales by weight — pill 4 → modal 12.
Calm, deterministic motion
Fades and soft slides, 120–320ms. No bounce, no infinite loops, all behind reduced-motion.
