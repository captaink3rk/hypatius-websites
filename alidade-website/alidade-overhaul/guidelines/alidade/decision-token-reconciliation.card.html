Decisions · resolved 16 Sep 2026

The ALIDADE token layers are reconciled.

Colour and geometry resolve to the brand layer. Elevation gets one written exception. Two token values are adopted from the live application because they measure better. The data-brand="alidade-hud" scope is retired. There is one ALIDADE palette, and tokens/alidade-v2.css is it.

Why colour goes to the brand

Both palettes clear WCAG AA in almost every role, so contrast alone does not decide this — it only rules out two specific values (below). The deciding argument is that the live app's accents are framework defaults nobody chose. #5eead4 is Tailwind teal-300; #fb7185 is rose-400; the radius scale is Tailwind v4 as shipped. The brand accents were tuned against the crystalline A mark's iridescence, and the mark is locked. A locked mark sets the field it sits on, not the reverse — so the field, the teal and the geometry are the brand's.

The elevation exception, and why it is narrow

The brand rule — depth is surface level plus border alpha, never shadow or blur — is right for content and it stays. It is wrong for exactly one case, and the live app was right to break it there: chrome that docks over scrolling content cannot separate from it by surface step, because the thing sliding underneath is also a surface. So elevation becomes a token with one permitted use, not a judgement call made per component.

Permitted on: the action/detail rail, the command banner, sticky headers, modals, popovers, the command palette. Not permitted on: cards, rows, KPIs, gates, watermark blocks, tiles, anything on a marketing surface. If it is not docked over moving content it gets --al-elev-0, and that is the end of the conversation.
