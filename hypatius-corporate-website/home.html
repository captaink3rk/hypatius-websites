Hybrid-architecture multi-domain operations

Edge to core, and back.

Five domains sense at the edge — the domain space. One hybrid layer, tolerant of denied, disrupted, intermittent, and limited (DDIL) links, carries it to the core — the information and decision space. There, threat becomes measured courses of action (MOMs · MOEs · MOPs), verified by an operator, and battle management, command, control, communications, and intelligence (BMC3I) is pushed back to the edge at the speed the threat level demands.
