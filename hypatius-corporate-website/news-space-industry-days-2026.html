HYPATIUS will be at Space Industry Days 2026

No booth and no keynote this year. The team behind STARCHITECT is in Los Angeles all week to talk shop, compare notes and meet the space community.

HYPATIUS will be in Los Angeles for the full week of AFCEA-LA Space Industry Days 2026, the annual gathering of Space Systems Command, government program offices and the industry teams that support them. Main sessions run October 21–22 at the Los Angeles Airport Marriott.

We are not presenting and we do not have a booth. We are there to listen, learn where programs are headed, and meet the people who build space systems: operators, program offices, primes and other small businesses.

If you work in space domain awareness, mission planning, wargaming or C2, we would like to hear what you are working on. We are also glad to walk through STARCHITECT, our space-domain wargaming and command-and-control simulation platform, over coffee or between sessions.

“Find us in the hallway, at a session or over coffee. If you are in LA for SID, let’s meet.”
