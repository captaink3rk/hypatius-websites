Why we exist

Good firms should win on merit.

Contested-domain defense is too high-stakes to run on guesswork and disconnected tools. The environment fights back — in orbit, across the spectrum, at sea, and along the contracting line that funds it all.

So we built one instrument that finds the opportunity, scores it honestly, charts the partner, and protects the work. Three platforms, one operating philosophy: we don’t build features — we engineer the decision.

Ninety percent accuracy is a one-hundred percent failure rate. We hold ourselves to the standard the mission demands.
