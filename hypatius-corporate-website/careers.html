How we work

Operators first. Always.

Four things we hold to — in the codebase and in the room.

01
Own the outcome

We don’t ship features and call it a day. You own the decision your code enables, end to end.

02

03
Earn the trust

Audit-grade by default. We work where mistakes are expensive, so we make them rare and recoverable.

04
Commercial cadence

We move fast on OTA, CSO, and SBIR Phase III vehicles. Shipping beats waiting on the next budget cycle.
