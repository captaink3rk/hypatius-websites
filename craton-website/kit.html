07 · Performance & technical SEO notes

  • Fonts: Big Shoulders Display 800, Rajdhani 500/600, IBM Plex Mono 400/500 — self-host, subset, font-display: swap.
  • Heroes as AVIF with JPEG fallback; LCP target under 2.0 s on 4G.
  • Structured data: Organization (HYPATIUS) + SoftwareApplication (CRATON, applicationCategory BusinessApplication). No AggregateRating until there are ratings.
  • Every image has alt text describing the object, never the brand ("Geological core sample with bronze seams", not "CRATON hero").
