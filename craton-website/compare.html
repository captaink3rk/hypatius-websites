Questions we get asked

The short answers.

Is CRATON DCAA-certified?
No software is. DCAA evaluates a contractor's accounting system — people, policy and tooling together — and finds it adequate or not. CRATON is built to the fourteen areas of the SF 1408 that evaluation uses.
